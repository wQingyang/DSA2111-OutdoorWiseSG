# Collector reliability and October 15 diagnosis

## Evidence and scope

This audit reads existing CSVs and the one saved worker-status JSON. It does not
start collectors, query environmental endpoints, initialize operational storage,
interrogate private system logs or alter collected data. Full per-route evidence
is in `snapshot_metric_audit.csv`; source attempts/counters are read directly from
`data/runtime/ingestion_runs.csv` and observation histories. Source CSV timestamps
are interpreted as Singapore local time. Numerical results are regenerated by
`analyze.py` and `reliability_audit.py`.

air quality still has usable evidence. There are zero blocked routes and 12

successful attempt at 12:07:05. Wind succeeds at 11:41:56 and next starts at 12:07:35;
WBGT succeeds at 11:15:58 and next starts at 12:05:49. The latest completed run

that intervening global interval. No rainfall/WBGT/PM2.5/PSI failures are recorded
in the preceding two hours; wind speed has one earlier transport failure followed
by successes. The logs do not establish a source-wide failed request at noon.

**Unconfirmed operational cause:** retained evidence cannot determine whether a
process was running at exactly noon, paused, waiting on work not yet logged, asleep,
disconnected, terminated by Terminal, or otherwise interrupted. Source attempts
are logged after completion; an interrupted request may leave no run row. Network
errors elsewhere do not explain every no-attempt interval. The single overwritten
worker report describes a later process (October 27), not October 15. No retained
historical process-event, Terminal or macOS sleep log resolves this ambiguity.
Upstream API failure, laptop sleep and process termination are not interchangeable.

**Evaluation logic:** revision-first-fetch, observation and provider-update
availability are applied before matching, using Singapore-local semantics. Tests
compare the reader's version selection to the production repository's pure method.
The as-of ledger explains unknown scores without a snapshot-selection bug. October 9
and 10 remain the original primary dates and each has 12 complete, usable route
assessments (5 eligible, 7 caution, 0 blocked/unknown); they were not replaced by
dates selected for stronger effects. Complete coverage can include approximate
station estimates and regional pollution; it is not proof of route-wide measurement.

## Polling history and attribution

Across all eight sources there are **11,330 recorded attempts: 11,204 successes and
126 failures**. Error categories are 111 transport and 15 parser/schema failures;
these do not establish government API outages. The previous Data Freshness
classification is reused unchanged: regular gap at most `2I+0.5` minutes,
extended gap greater than `4I+0.5`, where `I=5` minutes for station weather and
15 minutes for WBGT/air. The active label additionally requires the third or later
consecutive timely, short successful run.

| Source | Qualifying active successful runs | Runs with direct retained fresh versions | Runs with direct retained stale versions | Active runs with an age bound beyond the application freshness limit | Active runs demonstrably >60 min | Maximum active age lower bound |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| WBGT | 558 | 555 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 25.18 min |
| PM2.5 | 598 | 586 | 0 | 2 | 13 | 135.80 min |
| PSI | 594 | 582 | 0 | 2 | 12 | 136.27 min |

Direct-version counts demonstrate at least one retained measurement from a run;
they do not describe all records in that response and can overlap. Bounds use
complete first-introduction history reconciled with ingestion insertion counters;
they cover some unchanged responses absent from revision history. Unknown responses
are not filled in. Bounds are evaluated only on qualifying active runs, even in
the all-successful summary row. Application limits are 15/45/120/120 minutes;
>60 minutes is a separate diagnostic, not a provider SLA. Repeated old responses
may be one correlated episode and cannot be counted as independent API outages.

These results distinguish four observable situations: retained fresh measurements
in successful polls, retained stale measurements or demonstrated stale bounds in
successful polls, recorded failed attempts, and intervals with no completed polling
record. A fifth situation, a verified process outage, requires independent process
evidence that is absent historically.

## Production and UI behavior under unknown conditions

`EnvironmentalRiskAssessor` leaves risk null/incomplete and eligibility unknown.
`RiskAwareRanking` assigns no weighted score or environmental suitability to unknowns,
places them after complete assessments and retains the deterministic legacy fallback.
With the evaluation's no-location/no-forecast preferences, that fallback is distance
order, then route ID. An authenticated unknown group can use difficulty mismatch
instead; unknown never becomes a complete safety assessment. Explicit blocks, when
enabled, are still removed even from a partly unknown candidate set.

The API emits the no-usable-assessment warning and discovery explanation.
`frontend/results.js` shows “Assessment unavailable”, “Unknown”, incomplete-information
text and the non-environmental fallback message, with recommendation score unavailable.
The system does not silently assign zero risk. Users can still choose a discovery
route, so the UI disclosure is important; its presence is not a measured user
comprehension result. No safety-policy change was justified or made.

## Reliability improvement and limits

See [`docs/LOCAL_COLLECTOR_MACOS.md`](../../docs/LOCAL_COLLECTOR_MACOS.md) for the
optional per-user launchd configuration and explicit operator commands. It runs
the existing worker against host CSVs independently of Terminal, requesting restart
after unexpected exit while logged in/awake. Compose and Kubernetes already supervise
their own collectors; their manifests remain unchanged. No service is activated.

Additive JSON diagnostics distinguish startup/heartbeat/shutdown/failure events,
catchable stop signals, cycle timestamps, actual per-process successful fetch times,
logged ingestion-run finish and persistent failure counts. Stale `running` status
is never a process-liveness claim. SIGKILL cannot log its own termination; later
startup/PID and supervisor status provide additional evidence prospectively.
The fixture recovery test verifies killed-worker lease release, clean relaunch,
subsequent signal shutdown and unchanged temporary observation/history bytes.
Native launchd restart, real sleep/wake and reboot were not tested or activated.

Historical coverage is affected both by upstream observation availability and by
operational continuity of local collection. These factors must remain separate
when interpreting freshness and recommendation results. An always-on connected
host is necessary for unattended 24/7 coverage; a local LaunchAgent cannot collect
during sleep, logout or power-off and cannot fabricate missing history.
